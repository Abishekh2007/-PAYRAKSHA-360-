VH-S02 — Detecting Digital Payment Scams Before Money Is Sent Problem: Digital-payment scams increasingly rely on social engineering: fake customer-care interactions, impersonation, malicious payment requests, QR-code tricks, fake shopping pages, and urgency-based messages. Most systems react after a transaction or complaint occurs. Challenge: Build a privacy-conscious system that identifies suspicious signals before a user completes a potentially fraudulent payment. The solution could analyse combinations of user-provided messages, URLs, QR/payment-request metadata, transaction context, behavioural patterns, or other non-sensitive signals and provide an understandable risk warning. Expected Outcome: The goal is not simply spam detection; teams should demonstrate why the situation is suspicious and what triggered the warning.
